Six adjustable conditions; many possible encounters.

Illustrative traces, not measurements. A conceptual model for a proposed exhibition.

Sliders use arrow keys for small changes, Page Up and Page Down for larger changes, Home and End for the endpoints. The illustrated controls represent project arrangements, not measured psychological variables. Ho Chi Minh City remains unchanged while controls alter the illustrative output traces.
